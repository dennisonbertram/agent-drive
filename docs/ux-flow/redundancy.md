## Hierarchy & IA

Summary: The console is a tab shell with two exceptions that leave the shell: "Billing & limits" and "Activity & deletion" are separate pages with their own header and a differently labelled back button. The nav order and weight do not match task frequency, and several concepts carry two names. Count: 10 findings.

1. **Two navigation systems in one sidebar.** Files, Agents, API keys, Usage, Trash are in-page tabs under "Workspace / <tab>" breadcrumb (02-06). Billing & limits and Activity & deletion leave the shell: no sidebar, no breadcrumb, different header (07, 10). Back buttons disagree: "Back to workspace" (07) vs "Back to files" (10). Fix: keep both inside the shell, one breadcrumb pattern.
2. **Sidebar styling mixes "tabs" and "links".** "Billing & limits" has a `$` icon, "Activity & deletion" has no icon (02-06). The inconsistent icon marks the second as an afterthought. Add an icon or group them under a heading such as "Account".
3. **Group the nav.** Seven items under one "WORKSPACE" heading (02). Daily items: Files, Trash. Setup items: Agents, API keys. Account items: Usage, Billing & limits, Activity & deletion. Usage and Billing & limits are one topic split in two (05, 07; Usage's only unique content is the per-drive and per-author table, and its third card is a link to Billing). Merge Usage into Billing, or put Usage at top of Billing and drop the "EGRESS & COST" card.
4. **Agents and API keys are one concept split in two.** Agents lists identities with "2 active keys" (03); API keys lists the keys of those agents (04). Create-key requires choosing an agent, and "Revoke access" on Agents revokes keys that are managed on the other tab (03, 04). Merge into one "Agents" tab with keys nested under each agent (STORY-023, 027, 047, 048). This also removes the "Create agent" button from the API keys empty state.
5. **Connection guide is buried under keys.** "From your terminal" (04) is the first-run instruction for the product's main audience, shown below the key list, and uses a placeholder host "https://your-agent-drive-host". A new user's first task is to connect an agent; it should be on the first screen after sign-up (STORY-024).
6. **Trash tab is rarely used but top-level; deletion is under another page.** Trash sits in the main nav at the same level as Files (06) while "Activity & deletion" (10) holds the permanent deletion controls and the audit log. Move Trash to a view inside Files (a "Deleted" filter), which also removes the repeated drive selector and key loader on Trash (02, 06). Rename "Activity & deletion" to two things, or call it "Activity" and move deletion to Settings; "Deletion" next to an audit log is surprising.
7. **Drive selector per tab.** Drive selector, drive id and "Load drive key" repeat on Files and Trash (02, 06), and Usage re-lists drives in its own table (05). Make drive a workspace-level choice in the shell header.
8. **One concept, one name.** Workspace: "Personal workspace" (02-06), "Your workspace" (07) and "Your workspace, your control" (10), "Operator console" subtitle under the workspace switcher (02), "Human console" actor (05). Files vs "Drives & files" (heading, 02) vs "All files" (card). "Trash" (verb in rows, 02) vs "Deleted files" (06) vs "drive.trash" (10) vs `rm`/`delete` (CLI/MCP, STORY-060). "Encrypted storage" / "Retained storage" / "Stored ciphertext" (05, 07, 01). Pick one for each and use it everywhere.
9. **Landing and pricing navs differ for no reason.** Landing nav: "Pricing / Sign in" (00); pricing nav: "Quickstart / Sign in" (01). Neither has Home or the other's link; pricing links to "Quickstart" which does not exist in landing. Use one header on both pages. Signed-in users still see "Sign in" and "Create your workspace" on landing, per the lead observation (unverified in screenshots: 00 is the signed-out view). Show "Open console" and hide the other two when a session exists.
10. **Hero weight on screens that have none.** Several screens put eyebrow + H1 + subtitle + action (02-06, 07, 10) so every tab has the same large header, even Trash and Usage with nothing to act on; the Usage third card (05) is a button that crowds a caption. The Files drives list, the main task, starts below the key banner and drive selector (02), pushing the first file row to y=360 of 670. Tighten the header, and reserve the large H1 for pages with one primary action.

## What I would not change

- Share recipient page is minimal and clear (11).
- Version history dialog explains Restore consequences ("Restore creates a new current version", 13).
- Load-key dialog states the key is memory-only (12).
- CLI vs MCP differences in share expiry (2 min vs 60 s, STORY-038) are a bug, not a feature: pick one. Not a UX redundancy, but it is a duplicated rule.
