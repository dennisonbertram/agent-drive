# Landing (/) visual audit, production, 2026-11-03

Screenshots in `shots/`. Measures in `audit.json` (layout-audit + geometry-audit, per viewport). Second opinions in `second-opinion.json` (muse-wonder and gemini-4-argon both ran). No horizontal page overflow at any viewport (pageScrollX 0).

## Verdicts

- **1440x900** (`1440x900-default-1.png`, `-2.png`): clean and calm, but the hero is one left column. The right 45 percent is empty (audit: hero is 63 percent of the viewport with 11 percent fill). The three pillars only peek in at the fold as 9px eyebrows. Not excellent.
- **1920x1080** (`1920x1080-default-1.png`, `-2.png`): same layout in a 1084px container, so the empty area grows. The page is 1109px tall, so it scrolls 29px just to show the footer; the footer is sliced at the fold.
- **768x1024** (`768x1024-default-1.png`, `-2.png`): works. Pillars drop to three narrow columns and two headings wrap while one does not, so the row looks ragged.
- **375x812** (`375x812-default-1.png`, `-2.png`): the headline reflows to three lines and is fine, but the only product evidence, the CLI command, is clipped after `/memory/`. CTAs stack with different widths and are 40px tall.
- Interaction (`1440x900-focus-tab3.png`, `1440x900-hover-cta.png`): Tab order is logo, Pricing, Sign in, Create your workspace, Open console, which is logical. A 3px green ring with offset shows on every control. I could not see a hover change on the primary CTA (computed background unchanged right after hover; 0.15s transition, so treat as unverified).

## Excellent as is

- Wordmark, nav and hero alignment share one left edge; single high-emphasis primary button (H1).
- Warm cream and lime palette is restrained; accent reserved for the primary action.
- Pillar copy is short, plain and consistent (3 columns, numbered eyebrows).
- Logical tab order, visible focus ring on all controls, no horizontal overflow at 375.
- Headline reflows cleanly across all four viewports.
