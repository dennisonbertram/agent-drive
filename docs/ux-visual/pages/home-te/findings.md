## Verdicts

**1440x900** (`1440x900-hero.png`, `-games.png`, `-protein.png`, `-relay.png`, `-console.png`, `-pricing.png`, `-final.png`, `-faq.png`). Strong. One 5-line headline, one orange action, a two-column hero where the figure (460x370) sits beside the copy, hairline columns, no horizontal overflow (docW 1440), page 7,432 px tall. The three figures read as one family and their labels measure 11.0 px effective. Weak points: the human sign-up action is an outline button everywhere while the orange button copies a prompt string (H-01); the footer starts 8 px left of every section (H-04); about 184 px of empty band under the final CTA (H-15); figure labels in light grey on cream are 2.2:1 (H-13).

**768x1024** (`768x1024-hero.png`, `-relay.png`, `-games.png`, `-protein.png`, `-console.png`, `-pricing.png`, `-footer.png`). Reflows without overflow and keeps the full nav. Two real problems: the three-column "relay" row squeezes shell commands into 207 px columns that break inside paths (`/202` + `6-11-09.csv`, H-09), and the console screenshot swaps to the phone image only below 761 px, so the wide desktop image shows at about 30 percent scale with text near 7 px (H-10). The figures stop at 560 px and sit left, leaving a 144 px empty strip on the right (H-17). Footer is 8 px left of the page gutter again.

**390x844** (`390x844-hero.png` through `-footer-2.png`, interactions listed above). No horizontal overflow (docW 390), body text 16 px, figure labels 11.0 px effective, copy feedback ("Copied" in place, visible ring) works, menu opens as a 44 px popover and closes with Escape, FAQ rows are 63 px or taller and expand cleanly, focus rings are 2 px solid. The page is 11,555 px (13.7 screens); problem (1,275), relay (1,916), pricing (1,313) and footer (1,168) take 5,672 px, nearly half (H-02). Header has "Menu" and "Sign up free" at identical weight and the header scrolls away (H-03). The drive activity log loses its column headers and shows four unlabelled lines per row (H-07). 33 interactive elements are under 44 px (H-12). Figures are legible; protein and hero are crowded (H-14).

**360x780** (`360x780-hero.png`, `-games.png`, `-protein.png`, `-log.png`, `-pricing.png`, `-final.png`, `360x780-figA-*.png`). Same as 390 with a 320 px figure. Here the figures break first: in the protein figure "TOO BIG FOR GIT" touches the timecode, "0.00 / 2.6 GB" overlaps "0 IN GIT" (10 px vertical overlap, measured), and the scale labels "GIT 100 MB / 1 GB / 10 GB" are jammed; in the hero the tile numbers touch the agent names (13x4 px overlap) (H-14). The header drops the wordmark below 361 px and its brand icon sits at x=12 while all content sits at x=20 (H-27).

**Reduced motion** (390, `390x844-reduced-*`). The loops do not run (two captures 2.5 s apart are byte-identical for the hero). Each figure shows a finished frame (hero "DRIVE KEPT 22 FILES", games "16 ASSETS READY", protein "SAVED 3 FILES 7.20 GB"), which is a sensible static state. Two flaws: the hero's five agent tiles are drawn as dashed ghosts with 2.2:1 labels, which reads as disabled, and the timecodes freeze at 00:10.5, 00:11.0 and 00:09.5 as if a clock had stopped (H-21).

## Excellent as is

- Hero headline and structure: one 5-line headline, one short lede, the prompt panel visible above the fold at 390 and 360 (Copy at y=486).
- Copy feedback: the label changes in place to "Copied" with a visible focus ring; no toast needed (`390x844-copy-hero.png`).
- No horizontal overflow at 1440, 768, 390 or 360 (docW equals viewport at each).
- Figure label floor: all three figures measure 11.0 px effective at every viewport; the env switch (`envFor`) works as designed.
- The three figures share one panel chrome, one aspect ratio (520x420) and one palette, and loop clearly with a sensible hold; they pause offscreen and do not animate under reduced motion.
- Mobile menu: 44 px rows, closes with Escape, clear open state.
- FAQ: rows 63 px or taller, plus/minus indicator, 16 px / 26 px body, inline code styled.
- Focus: 2 px solid ring on every control tried, logical tab order (brand, Menu, Sign up, Copy, Copy, See how).
- "The problem" diagrams (struck-through "gone" state in a dashed box) explain the point without text.
- Hairline tables and columns at 768 and 1440 (pricing, features, compare, drive activity log).
- Section rhythm is uniform (84 px top padding, left edges of every h2 at the same x at all four viewports).
