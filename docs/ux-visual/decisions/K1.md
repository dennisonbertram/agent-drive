# K1: The three figures need a phone layout

Review note: one judge panel scored all three lenses (usability, visual craft, engineering). External MUSE Wonder and Gemini Argon judges were not run, so there is no external rebuttal.

## Problem

Each figure is a 520x420 desktop drawing scaled to about 320 px on a 360 phone (0.62). Labels then collide, grey labels are 2.23:1, and decorative codes (AD-01, S01, timecodes, unlabelled counters) add noise. Findings H-13, H-14, H-17, H-20, H-21, H-22, H-28 (`pages/home-te/findings.md`). The owner chose the Swiss grid + dot-matrix style; it stays.

Acceptance criteria (from `problems-home-te.md`): at 360 and 390 no figure text overlaps or touches other text or lines; every label is at least 12 px effective and at least 4.5:1; no decorative codes without meaning; reduced-motion frames look active; first paint uses the mobile size.

## Options considered

Criteria scores are the panel consensus (1 to 5). Weighted total is out of 60 (criterion 1 x3, criteria 2 to 5 x2, criterion 6 x1). Lens totals are each lens's own weighted score.

| Option | Idea | Meets | Simple | Consistent | Usable | Visual | Effort | Weighted | U / V / E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Minimal | Same 520x420 panel, 12 px floor, phone label variants, codes removed | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **53** | 52 / 50 / 56 |
| B Restructure | A second, taller 320-unit drawing per figure, both server-rendered | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 2 | 48 | 50 / 53 / 40 |
| C Convention | Full-bleed crop, words moved to an HTML key under each figure | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 39 | 38 / 42 / 41 |

All three score 4, not 5, on criterion 1 because none has run the geometry check; each says "checked by eye". A also misses H-17 and H-20 until the grafts below are added.

## Decision

Option A, with four grafts.

1. **Label floor and colours** (`apps/web/app/(app)/te-figure.tsx`): `envFor` uses `fs = Math.max(10, 12 / f)`. Add `C.ot = '#c2370f'` for orange text on cream. No `<text>` uses `C.g2` (`#a8a69f`); secondary text uses `C.g3` (`#6f6d68`). `C.g2` stays only for non-text marks (earlier-session tiles, registration crosses). Verified ratios on `#f6f5f2`: `C.g3` 4.74:1, `C.ot` 5.01:1; `#ff4a1c` on `#111` (dot-matrix strip) 5.62:1.
2. **Codes removed** (`chrome()` and the three figure files): no panel code (AD-01/02/03), no running timecode, no `S0x` on the strip, no agent numbers 01 to 05, no unlabelled folder counters, no angle readout. The strip's right side shows a labelled value ("21 FILES", "7.20 GB", "14 ASSETS") or nothing. Games "16×16 PX · GEN 00/04" becomes "DRAWING 3 OF 4". Protein "7XKA BACKBONE · Cα 055°" becomes "PROTEIN BACKBONE". SPR / TEX / SFX / LVL stay: the strip spells the action ("DRAW JUMP_04.WAV") and the audience is game developers.
3. **Phone label variants** in the existing `env.narrow` branch, exactly as A describes: hero keys 86 units wide, names left-aligned on one or two lines (OPEN / CODE, ANY / AGENT), handoff note "next.md" / "TO CODEX", legend EARLIER / THIS SESSION / HANDOFF, "ENCRYPTED AT REST" dropped on phones; games pills 30 units tall, meta row at y=347; protein axis shows only "GIT LIMIT" on the 100 MB tick, file rows at 62-unit pitch with the size on its own line under the bar.
4. **Reduced motion**: hero `still` moves from 10.5 to about 6.6 (handoff just saved, Claude key pressed, packet on the bus). No timecode exists to freeze.
5. **First paint**: `ENV0 = envFor(320)`. In `apps/web/app/styles/te-figures.css`, `.te-fig svg { --fs: 19.5px }` below 600 px and `10px` at 600 px and up. The component still sets the exact `--fs` inline after `measure()`.
6. **Graft (H-17, not in any proposal)**: `.te-fig { margin: 0 auto; }` so the 560 px figure centres in the 704 px column at 768 instead of leaving a 144 px strip on the right.
7. **Graft (H-20, not in any proposal)**: in `hero-figure.tsx` the key label colour switches at the midpoint instead of blending. Today `tc = mix(mix(C.ink, C.g0, press), …)` and `fill = mix(C.g1, C.ink, press)` pass through the same grey together, so the name disappears. Use `press > 0.5 ? C.g0 : C.ink` for the label before the `recv` and `off` mixes.
8. **Graft (H-22 data plausibility)**: rename the first protein file from `7XKA.pdb` (a PDB text file is not 1.4 GB) to `7XKA_map.mrc` (a cryo-EM density map, which is plausibly 1.4 GB). Keep the size. The owner can veto this; it is figure data, not page copy.

## Why this is the best

- **Against B**: B is the best-looking phone figure, but it is two drawings per figure to maintain forever, two server-rendered SVGs per figure (the protein dots roughly double), and each phone figure is 100 to 140 px taller than today. That works directly against K2, and the brief asks for the smallest change that meets the criteria. A's mocks (`mocks/K1-A-*-360x780.png`) already read cleanly at 360: names are legible, the protein size sits on its own line, the overlaps H-14 measured are gone. B's extra quality does not justify about three times the effort.
- **Against C**: full bleed breaks the 20 px gutter every other section keeps (K5 is about restoring that gutter, so C would add a new exception). Moving names to an HTML key means a reader must look from a glyph to a key below to know which agent is which. That is slower than reading the name on the key. The caption also adds 120 to 180 px per figure, so C does not help K2.
- **Grafts**: B's and C's genuinely better ideas (spelled-out asset types, readout-first protein) were considered. Spelled-out types do not fit A's pills at 12 px, and the strip already names the action, so they were not grafted. The three grafts above cover findings no proposal addressed.
- **Norms satisfied**: A1 (contrast), L6 (reflow without collisions), H6, C1 and C4 (no meaningless codes), A3 (reduced motion), L5 (first paint at the right size), L4 (centred at 768).
- **Costs and what it rules out**: the figures stay desktop compositions at 0.62 to 0.67 scale, so they read dense on phones. That is accepted to keep one drawing. The games "SPR" pill has about 4 px clearance at 360; tight but not touching. A desktop visitor's first frame uses the phone label variant at 10 px until `measure()` runs; it is valid, just sparser. Earlier-session tiles in `C.g2` are 2.23:1 as a non-text mark; the legend names the colour, so this is a residual risk, not a criterion failure. Rules out a separate phone drawing and full-bleed figures.

## Final acceptance criteria

1. At 360x780 and 390x844, for each of the three figures, at three moments in the loop (about 1, 5 and 8 s) and in the reduced-motion still: no two `<text>` bounding boxes intersect, and none intersects a key, pill or bar outline (1 px tolerance).
2. Every `<text>` in a figure renders at a computed size of at least 12 px (font size in units times the SVG's scale) at 360 and 390.
3. No `<text>` has fill `#a8a69f`. Text fills are limited to `#111111`, `#6f6d68`, `#c2370f`, `#ffffff` and the dot-matrix colours on `#111`.
4. No figure text matches `/AD-0\d|S0\d|\d\d:\d\d\.\d|Cα|GEN \d/`.
5. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the hero still shows no dashed key outlines and no timecode.
6. The server HTML (before hydration) for `/` contains the phone label variant (for example the hero key text "CLAUDE" without "01"), and `--fs` is 19.5px below 600 px.
7. At 768x1024 the figure's left and right gaps inside its column differ by at most 1 px.
8. At 1440 the figures look as today apart from the removed codes and the new colours (screenshot review).

## Implementation plan

1. `te-figure.tsx`: `C.ot`, `envFor` floor, `ENV0`, `chrome()` without code and timecode, title in `C.g3`.
2. `te-figures.css`: `--fs` per breakpoint, `.te-fig { margin: 0 auto }`.
3. `hero-figure.tsx`, `games-figure.tsx`, `protein-figure.tsx`: narrow variants, removed codes, strip values, label colour switch (hero), `still` 6.6 (hero), file rename (protein).
4. Screenshots at 1440, 768, 390 and 360, including reduced motion.

Order relative to the other clusters: K1 is independent of K2 and K3 and can ship first. K2's height estimates assume K1 keeps the 520x420 aspect ratio, which this decision does.
