# K3: No clear primary action for a human (with K4 and K5)

Review note: one judge panel scored all three lenses (usability, visual craft, engineering). External MUSE Wonder and Gemini Argon judges were not run.

## Problem

The only orange (filled) button on the page copies the agent prompt and is labelled "Copy". Every human sign-up action is an outline button, the header scrolls away after the first screen, and the sign-up action has five names. Findings H-01, H-03, H-05, H-25.

Original acceptance criteria: exactly one filled primary per screen region and it is sign-up for humans (the agent prompt stays prominent but secondary or clearly labelled); header sticky on phones with "Sign up free" as primary; one name for the sign-up action.

## Options considered

Weighted total out of 60.

| Option        | Idea                                                                                                                 | Meets | Simple | Consistent | Usable | Visual | Effort | Weighted | U / V / E    |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ------ | ---------- | ------ | ------ | ------ | -------- | ------------ |
| A Minimal     | Orange "Sign up free" in a sticky header, Copy becomes outline "Copy prompt", one name, pairing hint under the panel | 4     | 5      | 5          | 4      | 4      | 5      | **53**   | 50 / 52 / 57 |
| B Restructure | New numbered `StartSteps` block (paste prompt, then sign up) in hero and final CTA; icon menu                        | 4     | 3      | 3          | 5      | 3      | 3      | 43       | 47 / 40 / 42 |
| C Convention  | Hero button pair "Sign up free" + "Read the docs", tabbed install box (Your agent / Terminal)                        | 3     | 3      | 3          | 4      | 4      | 3      | 40       | 40 / 42 / 38 |

Claim checked against the code: `.landing` has `overflow-x: hidden` (`apps/web/app/styles/landing.css` line 16) and `SiteHeader` renders inside `main.landing` (`apps/web/app/(app)/page.tsx` lines 145 to 147). `overflow-x: hidden` forces `overflow-y` to compute to `auto`, which makes `main.landing` the sticky header's scroll container. That container never scrolls (it grows with its content), so `position: sticky` would do nothing on `/`. All three proposals' companion fix, `overflow-x: clip`, does not create a scroll container, so the claim is correct. On `/pricing` and the docs pages the header sits outside any overflow container; the docs sidebar is already sticky at `top: 76px`, which clears a 60 px header.

## Decision

Option A, with three changes to keep the owner's copy and two grafts.

1. **Header** (`apps/web/app/styles/site-header.css`): `.site-signup` gets `background: var(--sig, #ff4a1c)` at every width; delete the `.site-signup.signal` rule and its comment. `.site-header { position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 50; }`. Header markup unchanged ("Menu" text button stays; no icon menu).
2. **Sticky fix** (`landing.css`): `.landing { overflow-x: clip; }` instead of `hidden`.
3. **Graft (not in any proposal): anchors clear the header.** `html:has(.site-header) { scroll-padding-top: 72px; }` in `site-header.css`, so "See how", "See the FAQ above." and in-page links do not land under the sticky header.
4. **Hero and final Copy** (`page.tsx`, `SetupPanel`): `className="l-btn"` (no `signal`), caption "Copy prompt", and `.l-panel .l-btn { background: transparent; color: #fff; border-color: #fff; }` so it is a white outline button on the dark panel. Keep the accessible names "Copy agent instruction" and "Copy agent instruction again" (tests depend on them).
5. **Owner copy kept**: the panel tag stays "Give this to your agent" (A proposed "Paste this into your agent"), and the pricing heading stays "Start free." (a heading, not an action name).
6. **Pairing hint** (`apps/web/src/content/home.ts`, `HERO.next`): "Free to start. Your agent shows a pairing code, and you approve it in your account." This matches the real flow in `apps/web/app/llms.txt/route.ts` (the agent prints a code, the human signs in and clicks Approve). It also gives "Free to start." a reason to sit under the panel (H-25). Needs owner approval (see below). `/index.md` reuses the field.
7. **One name, "Sign up free"**: pricing Free button "Start free" becomes "Sign up free" with `signal`; final CTA link "Create a free account" becomes "Sign up free" with `signal`; footer `.sf-cta` "Create a free account →" becomes "Sign up free →"; `AuthLinks` in `site-header.tsx` "Create a free account" becomes "Sign up free". The "Developer" and "Team" cells stay outline or "Coming soon".
8. **Graft from B: the hero needs no second orange button.** B and C add a filled "Sign up free" in the hero, which on a phone puts two identical orange buttons in the first 600 px. With a sticky header, the filled "Sign up free" is already in every screen, including the first. The hero's focal point stays the owner's agent prompt, now with an outline "Copy prompt" and the pairing hint.

Revised K3 criterion, stated so it can be measured: every region (header, each section) has at most one filled button; every filled button on `/` is "Sign up free"; a filled "Sign up free" is visible in every viewport-height screen at 390 and 1440 (via the sticky header).

## Why this is the best

- **Against B**: B best explains the order (paste, then approve), but it replaces the owner's hero with new copy and a new component, makes the hero about 120 px taller at 1440 and 60 px at 390 (works against K2), and puts two orange "Sign up free" buttons in the first phone screen. A's one added sentence gives the same explanation without the restructure.
- **Against C**: C demotes the agent path, which is the owner's chosen hero, hides the terminal command behind a tab, uses an icon-only copy button, and also doubles the orange button in the first phone screen. "Read the docs" is a new action the owner did not ask for.
- **Norms satisfied**: H1 and H2 (one filled action per region, and it is sign-up), L2 and L7 (sign-up always reachable at the top), C1 and H5 (one name), H-25 (the free line now explains the next step).
- **Costs and what it rules out**: at 1440 the only filled sign-up is the 36 px header button, not a large hero button; a desktop visitor who wants to sign up looks top right, which is the convention. The sticky header takes 60 px (61 with its rule) of every screen. Rules out a hero button pair, a steps block and an install tab box.

## Final acceptance criteria (K3)

1. At 390x844 and 1440x900, after scrolling to `#pricing`, `#faq` and `.l-final`, the header's `getBoundingClientRect().top` is 0 and its "Sign up free" link is visible.
2. At 390x844 and 1440x900, in each viewport-height screen from top to bottom, every visible element with background `rgb(255, 74, 28)` is a link named "Sign up free", and no region contains two of them.
3. On `/`, no link or button text is "Create a free account", "Start free" or bare "Copy".
4. After clicking "See the FAQ above.", the FAQ heading's top is at least the header's bottom.
5. Hero and final Copy buttons keep their accessible names; the copy feedback ("Copied") still works.

## K4: Touch targets, link styles, menu alignment

Findings H-11, H-12, H-26, H-27. Criteria: every interactive target ≥ 44 px tall at 390 and 360; one link style per role; menu panel aligned to the page gutter.

**Decision: A's items 6 to 12, unchanged except where noted.** All three K3 proposals share these rules; B and C differ only in the header markup, which K3 does not adopt.

1. `.l-btn`: `min-height: 44px; padding: 0 18px; justify-content: center;` at all widths (replaces `padding: 10px 18px`, which gave 39 px).
2. `.l-copy` (terminal chip): `min-height: 44px; padding: 0 12px;`; on phones `.l-cmd { align-items: center }`.
3. One text-link style in landing content: `.l-also a, .feat > div > a, .l-pnote a { color: var(--i); font-weight: 500; text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-thickness: 1px; text-underline-offset: 3px; }`. Standalone links ("See full pricing") also get `display: inline-flex; align-items: center; min-height: 44px; align-self: flex-start;`. Inline links inside sentences ("See how", "See the FAQ above.") get `padding-block: 12px`. The compare links become block rows in K2, so the two compare link styles in H-11 disappear.
4. Footer links and CTA: `display: inline-flex; align-items: center; min-height: 44px;` and `.sf-cols ul { gap: 0 }`.
5. Header wordmark: `.site-header .brand { min-height: 44px; }`.
6. Menu popover: `inset: 64px 20px auto auto` (was `16px`), so the panel's right edge is on the 20 px gutter (x = 370 at 390).
7. At `max-width: 360px`, delete `padding: 0 12px` so the brand icon starts at x = 20 like the content. Keep the logo-only rule: with the "Menu" text button the wordmark does not fit in 320 px (B's fit calculation assumed a 44 px icon menu).

**Acceptance**: at 390x844 and 360x780, every `a`, `button` and `summary` on `/` (header, main, footer) and in the open menu has a bounding box at least 44 px tall; content text links share one computed style (colour, underline, weight); the open menu's right edge equals the content's right edge (±1 px); the header brand's left edge equals the content's left edge at 360.

**Test first (fails today)**: a Playwright test at 390x844 that lists every visible interactive element on `/` with height below 44 px and expects the list to be empty. Today it has 33 entries.

## K5: Footer gutter and type and spacing scale (minimal)

Findings H-04, H-06 (H-24 deferred). Criteria: footer edges align with page content at all four widths; ≤ 6 font sizes in landing and figure CSS; spacing values on the 4/8 scale.

**Decision.**

1. **Footer gutter** (`apps/web/app/styles/site-footer.css`): `.sf-wrap` horizontal padding becomes 32 px (was 24), and 20 px at `max-width: 760px` (was 16 px at 640). Keep the 1160 px max width. Footer content then starts at x = 172, 32 and 20 at 1440, 768 and 390, the same as `.l-wrap`.
2. **Type scale** (`landing.css` only; SVG figure labels are governed by K1): six roles, each used through a custom property on `.landing`: `--t-xs` 11 px (mono tags and labels), `--t-s` 13 px (small text, code; folds 12, 13.5 and 14), `--t-b` body 16 px on phones and 15 px at 761 px and up (folds 14 to 16; buttons use it), `--t-l` 18 px (ledes; folds 17, 19, 20, 22), `--t-h2` `clamp(32px, 2.4vw + 14px, 48px)` (all section and final headings; folds 34 to 48), `--t-h1` `clamp(44px, 4.5vw + 16px, 80px)`. The footer tagline uses `--t-h2`. The implementer adjusts the clamp slopes only if a heading wraps differently from today at 1440.
3. **Spacing**: in `landing.css` and `site-footer.css`, replace margin, padding and gap values of 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 14, 18 and 26 px with the nearest 4/8-scale value (4, 4, 8, 8, 8, 8 or 12, 16, 16 or 20, 24); ties round to the value that keeps the group spacing smaller than the space between groups. Borders, outlines and `text-underline-offset` are not spacing and stay.

**Acceptance**: `own.lefts` shows the footer's left edge equal to the h2 left edge at 1440, 768, 390 and 360; `layout-audit.js` reports at most 6 distinct font sizes per viewport on `/` outside the figure SVGs; `layout.spacing.offScale` is empty for `landing.css` and `site-footer.css` selectors.

**Test first (fails today)**: a Playwright test that compares the left edge of `footer .sf-wrap` content with `#problem h2` at 1440, 768 and 390. Today they differ by 8, 8 and 4 px.
